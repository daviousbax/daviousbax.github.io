LIVING CAVE

A reverse tower defense game for Android.

You are the Mother Gem, the living heart of an ancient cave. In 1863, greedy miners broke into your home hunting for Malibium. Command creatures, traps and spells to defend the cave through story chapters, endless waves and daily challenges.
