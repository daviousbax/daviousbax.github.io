Living Cave - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how the game Living Cave (package com.daviousbax.livingcave), made by Davious.Bax, handles your information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, profile name and optional profile photo are saved on your phone. A profile photo you choose from your gallery is cropped, shrunk and stored only inside the game's own storage. The game does not look at or upload any other photos.

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games display name and player ID with the game so it can link your progress. Your progress (including your profile name and photo, if you set one) is saved to Saved Games in your own Google account, so you can restore it on a new device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive or keep a copy of this data. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not show personalised ads, and we do not collect your contacts, location or other personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone. To delete your cloud save, open your Google Play Games settings and delete the game's saved data, or contact us and we will help.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
