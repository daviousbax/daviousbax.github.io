Living Cave - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Living Cave (package com.daviousbax.livingcave), made by Davious.Bax, handles your information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, profile name and optional profile photo are saved on your phone. A profile photo you choose from your gallery is cropped, shrunk and stored inside the game's own storage. The game does not look at or upload any other photos.

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games display name and player ID with the game so it can link your progress. Your progress (including your profile name and photo, if you set one) is saved to Saved Games in your own Google account, so you can restore it on a new device. We do not receive or keep a copy of your Google cloud save. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Online features (Unity Gaming Services)

If you use the ONLINE features, the game creates a player account with Unity Gaming Services. Your player ID, display name, profile (level, stats, badges and title), friends list, clan, leaderboard scores and the messages you send in chat are stored on Unity's servers so that other players can see them. If you choose to show your profile photo to other players, that photo is uploaded too.

You can make your profile private, or turn chat off, in the game's Settings. Unity's handling of this data is covered by the Unity Privacy Policy.

Reports and moderation

If a player reports a name, photo or message, the report and the reported content are sent to us so we can review it. Accounts that break the rules can be restricted or banned.

Purchases

Purchases in the game's shop are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only learn which item was bought, so the game can give it to you.

Play data (optional)

The game asks once whether you want to share play data. If you agree, it sends anonymous play data to Unity Analytics, such as when you play, how long your sessions last and what kind of device you use. It does not include your name, your chat or your photos. Nothing is sent unless you agree, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > SHARE PLAY DATA.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your own phone. Nothing is sent to us to do this, and you can turn them off in Settings > NOTIFICATIONS.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not show personalised ads, and we do not collect your contacts or your location.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone. To delete your Google cloud save, open your Google Play Games settings and delete the game's saved data. To delete your online account (your profile, friends, messages and scores) or any play data, email us with your player ID and we will delete it.

Children

Living Cave is intended for players aged 13 and over. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
