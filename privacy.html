Living Cave - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Living Cave (package com.daviousbax.livingcave), made by Davious.Bax, handles your information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, profile name, optional profile photo and local backups are saved on your phone. A profile photo you choose from your gallery is cropped, shrunk and stored inside the game's own storage. The game does not look at any other photos.

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games display name and player ID with the game. Your progress is saved to Saved Games in your own Google account, so you can restore it on a new device. That save contains your progress and settings, your profile name and photo, your play time, an identifier for the device that made the save (so the game can tell your phones apart), and the login for your online account (so a new phone gets the same account). We cannot see or copy your Google cloud save. Google's handling of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Your online account

When the game starts with an internet connection, it automatically creates an online account for you with Unity Gaming Services. You do not give an email address or a password. The account has a Unity player ID and, for installs from Google Play, a player number.

Your public card is stored on Unity's servers so other players can see it: your player number, name, title, badges, name colour, avatar, frame, level, best wave, story stars, miners stopped, rank points and clan tag. Your lifetime stats (games, wins, waves, bosses, chests, defenders placed, shards and gems earned, play time and favourite defender) are stored too, and shown when another player opens your profile.

If you turn on Settings > PRIVATE PROFILE, other players only see your name and player number, and your stats are not sent. Your scores still appear on the leaderboards.

The server also stores what the online features need: your friends list and blocked list, your leaderboard scores, your rank and match results, friend challenges and minigame matches, gifts sent to you, and whether the game was installed from Google Play. Once a minute the game tells the server that you are playing, so it can show how many players are online. Unity's handling of this data is covered by the Unity Privacy Policy.

Profile photos

Your profile photo is only uploaded if you choose it as your avatar and your profile is not private. It is shrunk to a small picture first. It is removed from the server when you stop using it or make your profile private. Other players can report a photo, and we can remove it or stop an account from sharing photos.

Chat and clans

Messages you send in EVERYONE chat, in private messages to friends and in clan chat are stored on Unity's servers so they can be delivered. Only the most recent messages are kept: about 60 in EVERYONE chat, 80 received and 80 sent private messages per player, and 50 per clan. Older messages are deleted as new ones arrive. Every message is filtered, and links, email addresses and phone numbers are hidden. Only your friends can send you private messages. You can turn chat off in Settings, and you can block any player.

If you create or join a clan, its name, tag and member list are stored and can be seen by other players.

Levels you share

If you share a level from the level editor, the level and your player name are stored so other players can find and play it. You can delete a level you shared.

Reports and moderation

If a player reports a photo or a message, we store which player was reported, how many times, and for chat the most recent reported messages, so we can review it. To keep the leaderboards fair, the server checks scores and may flag an account for review. Accounts that break the rules can be muted or banned from online play. A banned account keeps its offline progress.

Purchases

Purchases in the game's shop are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only learn which item was bought, so the game can give it to you.

Play data (optional)

The game asks once whether you want to share play data. If you agree, it sends anonymous play data to Unity Analytics, such as when you play, how long your sessions last and what kind of device you use. It does not include your name, your chat or your photos. Nothing is sent unless you agree, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > SHARE PLAY DATA.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your own phone. Nothing is sent to us to do this, and you can turn them off in Settings > NOTIFICATIONS.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not show personalised ads, and we do not collect your contacts or your location.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone. To delete your Google cloud save, open your Google Play Games settings and delete the game's saved data. To delete your online account and everything stored with it (your card, stats, photo, friends, messages, scores and shared levels), or any play data, email us with your player number and we will delete it.

Children

Living Cave is intended for players aged 13 and over. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
