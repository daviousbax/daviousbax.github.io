Living Cave: Delete Your Data

Living Cave is made by Davious.Bax. This page explains how to delete the data the game keeps about you.

What data Living Cave keeps

  • On your phone: your game progress, settings, player name and profile photo (if you chose one).
  • In your Google account (only if you linked it): a cloud save copy of the same progress, name and photo, stored by Google Play Games.
  • Player ID: your Player ID number is stored only inside your save. Our online counter only records how many Player IDs have been given out and holds no information about you.

Step 1: Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings and go to Apps.
  2. Tap Living Cave, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage (or simply uninstall the game).

Step 2: Delete your cloud save (Google Play Games)

On the web:

  1. Go to play.google.com/games/profile.
  2. Click Your Data, then Delete Play Games Account & Data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Living Cave and click Delete.

On Android:

  1. Open the Play Store app and tap your profile picture (top right).
  2. Go to Settings, General, Play Games Profile, then Privacy & Settings.
  3. Under Your Data, tap Delete profile & game data.
  4. Under Delete individual game data, find Living Cave and tap Delete.

What gets deleted

Following both steps deletes all of your Living Cave progress, settings, player name, profile photo and Player ID. Davious.Bax does not keep any other copy, so nothing is kept after you delete it.
